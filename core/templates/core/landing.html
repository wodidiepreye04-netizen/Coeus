Limits of confidentiality: Your privacy and trust are our highest priorities. All sessions and reflections are held in strict clinical confidentiality. However, in accordance with professional ethical standards and Nigerian law, confidentiality may be broken if there is an imminent risk of severe harm to yourself or others, suspected abuse of a minor or vulnerable individual, or a court order requiring disclosure. In such rare emergencies, our duty of care requires us to reach out to appropriate support services to protect human life.
