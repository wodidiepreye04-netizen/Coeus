Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2025

At COEUS, your privacy is not an afterthought — it is the foundation everything is built on. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how you stay in control.

1. What we collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the service:

  • Your email address (to create and secure your account)
  • Check-in responses and conversation data (to provide support)
  • Basic usage data (to improve the product)

2. What we never do

  • We never sell your data to anyone, ever.
  • We never share your data with your school, employer, or family without your explicit consent.
  • We never use your data to train external AI models without your permission.

3. Encryption

All conversations are encrypted in transit and at rest. We use industry-standard encryption protocols to protect your data.

4. Life-safety exceptions

If our support team believes you are in immediate danger, a senior counsellor may be notified. We will always tell you this before it happens, wherever possible. This is the only exception to our non-disclosure policy.

5. Your rights

  • You can request a copy of all data we hold about you at any time.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data at any time — permanently.
  • You can opt out of non-essential data collection at any time.

6. Data retention

We retain your data only as long as you have an active account. When you delete your account, all your data is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at privacy@coeus.ng.
